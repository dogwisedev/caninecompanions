About Me

My journey in dog training began eight years ago, born from a deeply personal mission to help others avoid the heartbreak of fear-based aggression. Since then, I have dedicated myself to mastering behavior modification, working with a diverse range of breeds and temperaments in both group and private settings. I believe that every dog has the opportunity to thrive when provided with a tailored, structured approach. By focusing on trust and effective communication, I help owners move past anxiety and reactivity to build a relationship that is grounded in confidence and lasting success.
