About Me

With over 14 years of professional experience and a Bachelor’s Degree in Animal Behavior, I focus on the science of how dogs learn and communicate. My career has been defined by my work with high-level service dogs—training and placing them with veterans and first responders to improve their quality of life. Whether I am preparing an adolescent service dog for placement or helping a family pet overcome behavioral challenges, I believe in patient, guided training that supports the mental well-being of both the dog and the handler.
