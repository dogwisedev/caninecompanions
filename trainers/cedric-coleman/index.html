About Me

With over eight years of hands-on experience, I have dedicated my career to mastering the complexities of canine behavior. Having served as a head trainer for multiple organizations, I’ve had the privilege of mentoring other professionals and turning "impossible" behavioral hurdles into success stories. My background in high-level protection sports, including earning my PSA PDC title, allows me to bring a unique level of precision and clarity to family obedience. I believe in empowering families to build deep, harmonious relationships rooted in trust and practical, real-world communication.
